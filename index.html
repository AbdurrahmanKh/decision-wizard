What the files look like
Round. Either a bare array of questions, or an object {config, questions} where config sets {title, tagline, product}. Each question: id, section, title, options (array of strings). Ids must be unique, and so must row ids inside one batch. Optional: question (the question itself, shown large, with the title as a small topic line above it; the sidebar keeps the title); context; detail (array of {kind: quote, note, or code, src, text}; code keeps indentation and line breaks and shows src exactly as written, such as a file path and line); links (array of {label, url}); extra ({label, blocks:[{kind: text, text}, {kind: table, title, columns, rows}, {kind: quote, src, text}, {kind: note, text}, or {kind: code, src, text}]}, or a list of them for several buttons) for context popups; customLabel; custom: false; skip: false; recommend (the label of the recommended option) with because (the reason, finishing "I recommend this because"), on a question, a follow-up, or a batch row. For a batch table set type: "batch" and items: [{id, label, note}], sharing one options set per row. Every question and every row takes a note with the pen. type: "rank" makes a ranking of 2 to 10 options (recommend is the whole order); "number" takes min, max, step, and unit instead of options (recommend is a number); "bracket" runs 4 to 32 options in knockout pairs, in the listed order. In config, needs_wizard names the oldest wizard version the round is made for, and a round written into a project sets project (the project's name) and folder (the full path of the folder the round file is in).
Brainstorm. An object with config.kind set to "brainstorm", a topic (a title and a question, and maybe context, detail, links, and extra, as a question has), and the cards of one of its two rounds. Round 1 has hold (the six hats, Blue first, each with an optional question), sparks (optional: a hat with an optional line and up to three sparks {id, text}), suggest (the six hats, Blue last, each with a line and up to four ideas {id, text, why}), and an optional recap {line}. Round 2 has aims (Blue's line, your goals, what to avoid, and said, your answers from round 1 that weren't ideas, each {hat, question, answer}), optional extras {line, ideas: [{id, hat, text, why, council}]}, ideas [{id, text, by, hat, from, council}], where by is "you", the hat that suggested it, or "merged" (then merged lists the cards it came from, each {text, by, hat}), and council holds up to four {hat, say} per hat, and an optional order {line}. Ids use letters, digits, - and _, and are unique; ids like u1 are kept for your own cards. A brainstorm saves, backs up, and works with project folders as a round does. A file made for wizard 0.8.0, with a hats list, no longer loads; one already in your list keeps its answers.
Saved file. What Save writes: the answers JSON (meta and answers, the same as Copy answers JSON) with the whole round under round, so opening it picks up where you left off.
Answers JSON. Answers on their own merge into the round with the same title, or into the open round: matching ids overwrite, and open entries are ignored unless they carry a note. A note comes back as comment.
Backup. What Back up all rounds writes: every round in this browser with its answers, each in the saved-file form, under rounds. Loading a backup adds the rounds that are missing here and brings back the ones the backup has a newer copy of. When a round changed here after the backup was made, the wizard asks whether to keep it.
Project folders. When a round names its folder, the wizard offers to save your answers into it. Pick the folder once, in Chrome or Edge on a computer, when a round asks or with Open a project folder. From then on your answers are written into the round's file as you go, in the saved-file form, and a new round landing in that folder opens by itself, or waits in the list while you are partway through another.
The list. Every round stays in this browser, with its answers, until you delete it. A round whose title is already in the list replaces the saved one, and answers to questions that did not change stay.

Wizard recommendations
When a round recommends an answer, a small wizard hat marks it, with the reason under it when there is one.
Where the context shows
Context is the framing, quotes, notes, links, and popups a question comes with. Hide, on its panel, folds it on that question, and Show brings it back.
Number keys pick answers
Keys 1 to 9 pick an answer while you're on the question, and 1 and 2 pick in a bracket. Shift with a number picks the answer and opens its note instead of moving on. They never pick while you type.

This round's clock
The clock starts when a round is first loaded and stops once every question has an answer. It pauses while your companion naps, and while you have another round open.
